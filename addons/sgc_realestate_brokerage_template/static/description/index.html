SGC Realestate Brokerage Template

Reusable, audit-driven template for real-estate brokerage tenants. The template is a tenant skeleton (per-tenant record, kit selection, preflight, audit-safe defaults) — not a full ERP.

What it gives you

  • Per-tenant sgc.brokerage.tenant with enablement flags.
  • Three pre-baked kits: brokerage_core, offplan_rental, growth.
  • Audit-driven preflight (M1, M3, M5, M0) gating tenant activation.
  • Audit-safe ir.config_parameter defaults — no hardcoded tenant workspace-account, contact email, or localhost fallback values.

What it does not depend on

Per the SGC audit 30_QUARANTINE/ hold list, the template's depends list explicitly excludes:

  • ks_dynamic_financial_report
  • sgc_crm_dashboard
  • sgc_lead_scoring
  • sgc_rental_management
  • sgc_construction_management
