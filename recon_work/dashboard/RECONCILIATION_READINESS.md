## 5. RECONCILIATION MECHANICS (how the pieces join)

```
incoming invoice  ──►  account.move  ──►  installment.invoice_id
                                            └─► state computed from invoice payment_state
                                                (pending/invoiced/paid/partial/overdue/cancelled)

incoming payment  ──►  matched to installment by contract_id + amount + payment_date
                       (312 paid lines currently have no date — see E2)

sale.contract.total_paid ──► balance_due ──► dashboard KPIs
escrow.release.journal_entry_id ──► account.move   (escrow releases, none yet)
```

**Watch-out:** `_compute_total_paid` sums *all* paid installments and `balance_due = sale_price − total_paid`.
When invoices arrive and lines switch from `pending` to `paid`, both recompute automatically via
`@api.depends` — no manual refresh needed. The 61 summary contracts will keep their lump-sum
"collected" line, so their `total_paid` already reflects history.
